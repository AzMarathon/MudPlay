Bundled image emotes
=====================

The image emotes in this folder are a "Pepe" community emote pack sourced from an
emoji.gg pack and supplied by the project maintainer for redistribution with
MudPlay. They are downscaled (max 128px) copies of the originals.

These render inline in the Conversation window when a message contains their
shortcode (e.g. :sadge:, :copium:, :monkas:) and the "Show emoji / emotes in the
conversation window" setting is on (Settings -> Talk).

The built-in Unicode emoji shortcodes (:lol:, :), :( , etc.) are plain Unicode
characters defined in code (Game/Emotes/EmoteCatalog.cs) and ship no image assets.
